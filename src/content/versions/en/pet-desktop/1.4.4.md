> This is an older version. Installed copies update themselves to the newest release; to start fresh, download from the product page.

## Highlights

- **Battle balance:** tuned skills, weapons, enemies and the Slime King; many new cards; Infinity offers cards more often and adds synergies at 6 and 9 cards of a group.
- **Battle malware:** slimes sometimes throw a loading ring that slows the cat, a "forced update" window that walls it off, or a self-copying virus.exe.
- **Adventure Guide:** the Adventure is now where you learn to play; at the right moment the battle pauses and highlights what to do. Skip is available.
- Battle apps can be turned off with a "Battle apps" chip in the setup panel.
- Bag synergies get icons, progress rings and descriptions.
- **A fresh look:** illustrated panels, 19 new icons and clearer battle effects.
- Pet sounds are on for new installs; the installer and download page also speak Japanese.

## Records

Adventure and Infinity records are kept separately under the new rules; old records stay. An Expedition run in progress is not affected.
