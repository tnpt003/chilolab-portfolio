## Highlights

- **Install with `PetComputer-win-Setup.exe`** (no admin rights, Desktop/Start menu shortcuts) and **self-update** from GitHub Releases. Your data is untouched by updates or uninstalling.
- **Infinity mode:** endless waves that grow stronger, a card every 3 waves and the Slime King every 10, with milestone rewards.
- **A battle hub** with three modes: Adventure, Expedition and Infinity.
- **Expedition:** spin to reveal each step's stops, a capsule machine after won fights, and one re-spin per step.
- A draggable battle HUD; setup panels scroll on short screens.
- The keepsake and keepsake shelf become toys: lift, throw and bounce them, and the cat reacts.
- New holidays: New Year's Day, March 8 and October 20.
