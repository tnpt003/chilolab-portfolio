## What is this version about?

1.5.0 focuses on **Battle Mode**, adding ways to interact with slimes and expanding the adventure. Battle Mode remains optional and off by default; outside a battle the companion cat is unchanged.

## Highlights

- Pick 5 of 10 skills in the setup panel, including five new mouse skills: laser pointer, fan, grabber, scissors, and hot water spray.
- **Cat fury:** a ring around the portrait fills up; when full, the cat grows for 5 seconds and shrugs off every harmful effect.
- Two new weapons: yarn and glove.
- 20 new slime types, each with its own counter, plus 9 team formations.
- Three new bosses: the Zip Slime, the Slime Mother, and the Mutant Slime.
- Poison, freeze, and sticky effects, and collectible drops (fish, potions, coins).
- Chapter 2, **The Download Dump**, with five stages unlocked after beating the Slime King.
- A second Expedition route, cat shops, new events and relics; rotating bosses and Mutations in Infinity.
- A fourth card group, **Trickery**, new malware, a Monster Book, 20 achievements, and Custom Fight.

## What stays

Chapter 1's five stages and your old records are unchanged. Infinity and the first Expedition route keep records separate under the new rules; old records are preserved.

## Known limitations at release

- Crowded battles may still drop frames.
- Full high-DPI/multi-monitor and upgrade-from-1.4.4 acceptance testing is still outstanding.

## Installing

Download `PetComputer-win-Setup.exe` and run it (no admin rights needed). If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**; the installer is not code-signed yet. Installed copies update themselves, so there is no need to download again.

Full notes (Vietnamese, English, Japanese) are on the GitHub release page.
