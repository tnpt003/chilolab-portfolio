## Highlights

- **Japanese:** the whole interface, battle data, coats/accessories and the cat's speech.
- **Battle apps:** five game-drawn app icons (Tab Tornado, Ping Storm, End Task, Ctrl+Z, Bin) charge up and help the cat. Slimes can infect an icon so it works against the cat.
- **A shorter right-click menu**, grouped by purpose; **Customize** is now its own window.
- Livelier landings; bringing the cat to the main screen comes with a puff.
- Volume applies while you drag the slider.
- Pause freezes the cat's poses too; leaving an Expedition battle keeps the run and its HP.

## Fixes

A records file holding `null` no longer causes an error; data is saved more safely. Adventure and Infinity records are kept separately under the new rules.
