## What is this version about?

1.5.2 is a small release for **the farm and the guide**: new players know what to do next and where coins come from, and the pet visits every land. It is also the first release from the new release home; installed pets move over by themselves, nothing to do.

## Highlights

- **What to do next in the field:** a ripe tile says "Ripe! Click to harvest", and after harvesting you are reminded to sell at the Store for coins. The field's right-click menu is shorter, with "Tend the field".
- **Where coins come from:** the guide spells out till → sow → water → harvest → sell at the Store & shop; the Store gives a hint when you are low on coins, and seeds you cannot afford say so.
- **"New" badge** on crops and dishes just unlocked, until you have seen them.
- **Chores spread more evenly:** the pet visits the coop for eggs, the pond and the hearth more often; a visit to the scratching post, plant or box is no longer skipped when the pet is busy.
- The intro unfolds open lands one by one from the farm sign; on small screens a land folds away instead of overlapping.
- The stall and the neighbor cat arrive on the same screen as the farm sign.
- Every step of the guided tour has its own picture, and a step stays when its thing is on another screen.

## Fixes

- The stall shows an egg for egg orders; the Store's "Another hen" row shows a hen.

## Known limitations at release

- Other DPI settings and multiple monitors have not been fully checked.
- New Japanese text has not been reviewed by a native speaker.

## Installing

Download `PetComputer-win-Setup.exe` and run it (no admin rights needed). If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**, because the installer is not code-signed yet. The installed app updates itself; you do not need to download it again.

Full notes (Vietnamese, English, Japanese) are on the GitHub release page.
